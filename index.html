TD3 stereo guided braking in CARLA

The cyan centerline comes from camera-detected lane markings. Stereo estimates the range to the parked vehicle. Watch the control indicator switch from TD3 to the deterministic brake override as the car approaches. The range plot shows the camera-estimated distance to the parked vehicle.

TD3 on left and right bends in CARLA

The first 20 seconds show the left bend. At 20 seconds the right bend begins and the simulation clock restarts. The speed trace comes from the camera, and the signed steering trace shows the command applied at each frame.

Geometric vision on left and right bends

Follow the yellow crosses across the rectified camera views. Their horizontal separation in pixels determines the displayed depth in metres. Black in the depth panel means the matcher found no valid correspondence.
