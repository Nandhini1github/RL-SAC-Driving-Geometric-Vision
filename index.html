How closely does each car follow the lane?

During inference, each trained SAC policy uses camera measurements to steer and accelerate. The gray line is the lane center and the teal line is the path driven. On this route, pure pursuit averaged 0.24 m tracking RMSE, adaptive SAC 0.25 m and fixed SAC 0.29 m.
